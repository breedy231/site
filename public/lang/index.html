How to run a session

Blocking your time

Twenty-five minutes, five days a week, same slot — the structure does most of the work. Protect it on the calendar and the rest follows.

12:15, 25 minutes

Put it on the calendar

Weekday lunch, every day, same time. Treat it like a standing meeting — once it's a habit instead of a decision, it survives busy weeks.

Bumped? Walk-home audio

The fallback

Meeting ran long, lunch got eaten by something else — any 🎧 task on the walk home still counts. The streak cares about minutes in your ears, not where you were standing.

Saturday is for talking

The longer session

Saturday is the exchange call plus whatever longer input you didn't get to during the week. It's the one day built for more than 25 minutes.

Conversation survival kit

O sea…oh SEH-ahI mean… / that is to say…
Pues…pwaysSo… / well… — the all-purpose filler
¿Cómo se dice…?KOH-mo seh DEE-sehHow do you say…?
¿Me lo repites, por favor?meh lo reh-PEE-tes por fa-VORCan you repeat that, please?
Más despacio, por favormas des-PA-syo por fa-VORSlower, please
No me acuerdo de la palabrano meh a-KWER-do deh la pa-LA-braI don't remember the word
Es decir…es deh-SEERThat is… / in other words…
A ver…ah verLet's see… — stalls while you find the words

The toolkit

What to use, and for what

Españolistos

Free · podcast

Two Colombian hosts talking at a natural, intermediate pace — the daily audio slot for most of Phase 1 and 2.

españolistos.com →

Coffee Break Spanish

Free · podcast

Leveled seasons — start at season 3+ to match a rusty-but-real base, rather than at episode one.

coffeebreaklanguages.com →

Dreaming Spanish

Free on YouTube

Comprehensible-input video, free on YouTube — the premium tier isn't needed to get real value out of it.

dreamingspanish.com →

Radio Ambulante

Free · NPR

Native-speed narrative journalism with free transcripts — the Phase 3 daily driver, once drills are done.

radioambulante.org →

SpanishDict

Free · grammar

The grammar refresher — preterite vs. imperfect, subjunctive, commands, si-clauses. Look it up, drill it, move on.

spanishdict.com →

Anki

Free · spaced repetition

A free top-frequency Spanish deck, 10 new cards a day — the vocabulary layer underneath everything else.

apps.ankiweb.net →

Tandem / HelloTalk

Free · voice exchange

Free language-exchange apps — the weekly 30-minute voice call (15 Spanish / 15 English) that makes speaking actually happen.

tandem.net →

Meetup.com intercambios

Free · in person

Chicago has free Spanish-practice meetups roughly every four weeks of this plan — the highest-pressure, highest-payoff practice there is.

meetup.com →

Netflix, Spanish subtitles

You already pay for it

Spanish audio, Spanish subtitles (not English) — Club de Cuervos, La Casa de Papel, and the rest for weekend immersion.

netflix.com →
